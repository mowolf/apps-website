Privacy Policy

Time Delta · effective 11 September 2026

Time Delta is a watch-accuracy tracker for iPhone, published by Moritz Wolf.

The short version

Time Delta collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network access of any kind. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere, because the app has no ability to send anything anywhere.

Data the app collects

None. We do not collect, receive, store or have access to any information about you or your device. The developer has no server, no database and no way of knowing who has installed the app or how it is used.

Data the app stores on your device

Time Delta saves the following in its own storage area on your iPhone, so it can show you your measurements the next time you open it:

  • The names and colors you give the watches you track
  • Each reading: the time your iPhone recorded, the time the watch face showed, and the time zone you were in when you took it

This information stays in the app's private container on your device. It is not transmitted, shared, sold or otherwise disclosed. Deleting the app removes it.

If you have iPhone backups enabled, this file is included in your own backup to iCloud or your computer, in the same way as other app data. Those backups are your own, controlled by your Apple Account settings; the developer has no access to them.

Tracking

Time Delta does not track you. It does not use the Advertising Identifier, does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising code.

Permissions

Time Delta requests no permissions. It does not use location, the camera, the microphone, contacts, photos, health data or motion data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or gains any network feature, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact
