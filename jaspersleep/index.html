JasperSleep

A baby monitor made of two iPhones.

In development

The phone in the nursery listens. Your phone stays silent until the baby has actually been crying for a while — then it wakes you, plays the room, and gets louder the longer the crying goes on.

What it does

The point of the app is what it doesn't do: it does not beep at every creak of the cot. Nothing happens until the room stays above the loudness you set, for the number of seconds you set.

  • No server, no account, no internet. The two phones find each other on your own Wi-Fi and talk directly, encrypted with your pairing code.
  • The audio never leaves your home. Sound is only streamed while the crying threshold is met, and only to your other phone.
  • An alarm that escalates. It starts quietly and grows, so a short grumble does not become a household emergency.
  • Listen in whenever you want — the parent phone can open the room on demand.

Privacy

Nothing the microphone hears ever reaches a server. See the privacy policy.
