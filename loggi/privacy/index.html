Privacy Policy

Loggi · effective 17 September 2026

Loggi collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network access of any kind.

Data we collect

None. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere, because the app has no ability to send anything anywhere. The developer has no server, no database and no way of knowing who has installed the app or what they log.

Data stored on your device

Loggi saves everything you create — your categories, their attributes and colours, and every entry you log with its date and values — in the app's own private storage area on your iPhone. This is the whole point of the app, and it stays on the device. It is not transmitted, shared, sold or otherwise disclosed. Deleting the app removes it.

If you have iPhone backups enabled, this data is included in your own iCloud or computer backup, like other app data. Those backups are yours, controlled by your Apple Account settings; the developer has no access to them.

Because some people use Loggi to track health-related things, it is worth being explicit: those entries are ordinary app data on your own phone. They are not sent to the developer, to Apple Health, or to anyone else.

Tracking

Loggi does not track you. It does not use the Advertising Identifier, does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising code.

Permissions

Loggi requests no permissions. It does not use location, the camera, the microphone, contacts, photos, health data or motion data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app stores or gains any network feature, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
