Loggi

Keep track of anything.

In development

A small iPhone app for logging whatever you like — symptoms, coffees, headaches, mileage. No accounts, no network, no sync.

What it does

  • Your own categories. Give a category a name, a colour and the list of things it records: numbers, text, a scale, a yes/no, a choice from a list, or a point dropped on a picture.
  • One tap to log. The three categories you use most fan out from the + button, so the usual entry is two taps.
  • Analytics. Everything logged against a category rolled up over a period — sums and averages for numbers, counts per option for lists, every point ever dropped plotted on its picture.
  • Filters. Stack conditions like Pain level > 5 or Took medication is Yes and see only the entries that match.

Privacy

Everything you log lives in a local database on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.
