GeoDrone

Airspace map with flight restrictions.

In development

The German UAS geo zones under § 21h LuftVO on a map — the areas where a drone flight is restricted or needs permission.

What it does

  • All 31 zone layers drawn as vector shapes and coloured by category: airspace, built-up areas, authorities, infrastructure, nature.
  • Choose what you see. Switch layers on and off by category so the map shows only what matters for your flight.
  • Works offline. Zones you have loaded are kept on the device, so the map looks the same with or without a signal.
  • Official source. Data comes from the open geo services of DFS Deutsche Flugsicherung (dipul). No API key, no registration.

GeoDrone is an aid for planning, not an authorisation. Responsibility for a flight and for checking the current legal situation remains with the pilot.

Privacy

Your location is only used on the device and never transmitted. See the privacy policy.
