Privacy Policy

The Sheep · effective 17 September 2026

The Sheep collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. The developer has no server and no database, and receives no information about you, your device or how you play.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your world — the terrain seed, where your sheep is, what you have discovered and your settings — in its own private storage area on your device, so you can carry on where you left off. This never leaves the device. Deleting the app removes it.

If you have device backups enabled, that save is included in your own iCloud or computer backup like any other app data. Those backups are yours and the developer has no access to them.

Tracking

The Sheep does not track you. It does not use the Advertising Identifier, does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Permissions

The Sheep requests no permissions. It does not use location, the camera, the microphone, contacts, photos, health data or motion data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app stores or gains any network feature, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
