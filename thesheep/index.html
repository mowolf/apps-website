The Sheep

A quiet exploration game for iPhone and iPad.

In development

Guide one sheep through a wide, generated landscape, discover companions, and learn to live together in the wild.

What it is

There is no score and nothing to lose. The world is made of irregular biome regions — meadow, forest, desert, mountains, jungle, arctic — that fade into one another rather than meeting at a line, and it is the same world every time you come back.

  • Tap to walk, hold to keep walking; the camera follows.
  • Each climate hides one landmark: a hill grave, an old hut, a pyramid, a cave, Maya ruins, a great icicle. All but the first are guarded, and a guardian has to learn to tolerate you before its door opens.
  • Every landmark can be entered. Inside it is dark — you need the torch from the altar outside to see anything between the entrance and the lit chamber at the end.
  • Ambient sound, rain, and no interface in the way.

Privacy

The game keeps your world on your device and collects nothing at all. See the privacy policy.
