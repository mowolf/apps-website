Releases

Model & property release forms for photographers.

In development

Fill in and sign model and property releases on your iPhone, right at the shoot. Each release is saved as a PDF on the imageBROKER.com release forms.

What it does

  • Model and property releases filled in step by step: model or property, photographer, shoot details, signatures.
  • Signed on screen. The model, owner, photographer and an optional witness sign with a finger or Apple Pencil.
  • A library you reuse. Models, properties and photographers are kept for the next release; a photographer's signature can be kept on file.
  • Date and location restrictions. Enter a shoot day or period and the country or region, or grant the release without restrictions.
  • Finish later. Unfinished releases are kept as drafts, for example until the second signature is in.
  • Your files, in iCloud Drive. Finished PDFs are saved to iCloud Drive › ReleasesApp so you can open them on your Mac and iPad, or kept on the iPhone only.

Releases is made for photographers who license their work through image agencies such as imageBROKER.com.

Privacy

Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud Drive. Nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.
